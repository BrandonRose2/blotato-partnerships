# Slack community answers

1. Pull threads from the partner channels that have had no reply for 2 hours or more (Slack MCP).
2. Sort each one:
   - **How-to** (API, MCP, templates, scheduling): answer from the Blotato docs at help.blotato.com and link the exact page.
   - **Payouts or commissions:** answer from the affiliate docs (50% of the first payment, NET-15 payout via Tolt). Anything account-specific goes to a human. Never guess a payout amount.
   - **Bug or outage:** acknowledge it, collect the steps to reproduce, and escalate to support.
   - **Win or milestone:** celebrate it in #wins and tag it for the scorecard.
3. Draft replies in a friendly, short, specific tone. Post how-to answers directly. Send payout and bug answers to a human for review first.
4. Once a week, turn the 5 most-asked questions into a FAQ post or a template, so nobody has to ask them again.
