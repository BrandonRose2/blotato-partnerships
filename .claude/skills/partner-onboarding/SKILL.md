# Partner onboarding: first 10 referrals in 30 days

## Inputs
- Partner name, type (creator or agency), main platform, audience size, niche
- Their Tolt referral link

## Steps
1. **Research (5 min).** Read their last 5 posts or videos. Note the format that gets the most engagement and the problem their audience keeps asking about.
2. **Write the welcome DM** (Slack or email, under 120 words): one specific thing you liked in their content, their referral link, and one concrete first action. No generic "excited to have you!".
3. **Build the 30-day plan** around their best format:
   - Week 1: one "how I automate my posting" post or video showing Blotato in their real workflow
   - Week 2: a free template or workflow (n8n, Make or Claude Code + Blotato MCP) gated by their link
   - Week 3: a before/after result post (hours saved, posts shipped)
   - Week 4: a live session or Q&A, then a pinned comment with their link
4. **Hand them a content kit:** three hooks written in their voice, a demo script, and the best Blotato templates for their niche.
5. **Set checkpoints:** day 7 (first post live?), day 14 (first referral?), day 30 (on pace for 10/month?). Add them to the scorecard.
6. **If at-risk** (21+ days, 0 referrals): offer a 15-minute call, find the blocker (time, confidence, wrong audience, technical), and fix that one thing.

## Output
A message draft, a 30-day plan, and a content kit, saved to `partners/<partner-slug>.md`.
